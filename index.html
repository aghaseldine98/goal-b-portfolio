The review of GAP

Gap reported its third quarter on 19 November. Sales rose 2%, profit beat its own plan, Old Navy steadied at flat comparable sales and the Gap brand grew 9%. Management cut its full-year forecast slightly after a warm-weather slow start to the holiday quarter and because it now assumes higher import tariffs. None of the conditions we set in advance for rethinking the position were hit. The lower forecast is essentially the profit level our own research had already assumed. We are holding the position at 9.3%. We are not adding, because the tariff outlook and analyst estimates are moving the wrong way, and we are not selling into the rally, because the stock still looks cheap against its earnings and cash. We are watching holiday sales, Old Navy under its new CEO, the tariff rate, and next year's guidance in the Q4 report.
